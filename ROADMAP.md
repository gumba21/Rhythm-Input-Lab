# Rhythm Input Lab roadmap

## Current stable release

### 4.5 — Cleanup and visualizer polish

- clean repository and versioning
- hurt-note and hazard support
- song mechanic categorization and analyzer
- precise seeking, combo HUD, audio synchronization, and Reports page

## In development

### 4.6 — Practice update

- section and custom-range looping
- chart-section navigation
- bookmarks saved per song
- previous/next miss navigation
- 50%, 75%, 90%, 95%, and 100% practice-speed presets
- section grades and range statistics
- weakest-section and last-miss practice ranges
- simple speed and loop recommendations

## Planned

### 4.7 — Analysis update

- lane heatmaps
- miss-cause analysis
- strongest and weakest sections
- fatigue detection
- pattern analysis
- attempt-history improvements
- song-completion analysis

### 4.8 — Profile update

- saved game, keybind, and judgment profiles
- FNF, osu!mania, Quaver, and custom presets
- lifetime statistics and improvement tracking
- strongest and weakest mechanics and lanes

### 4.9 — Quality-of-life update

- visualizer and interface polish
- settings, importer, and report improvements
- accumulated bug fixes and smaller requests
- deferred practice and sandbox-like tools that do not justify their own major update

### 5.0 — Universal rhythm engine

- a versioned Rhythm Input Lab chart format used as the internal interchange model
- import and export for FNF, osu!mania, Quaver, Clone Hero, StepMania, and RIL
- export back into FNF-compatible charts
- turn recorded attempts into draft charts
- combine multiple chartless attempts to reconstruct a higher-confidence chart
- preserve holds, hazards, labels, events, lanes, timing, metadata, and unsupported-mechanic warnings where possible

### 5.5 — Chart Doctor

- timing and audio validation
- impossible-transition and difficulty-spike detection
- conversion warnings and mechanic validation
- chart repair, completion, and difficulty estimation

### 6.0 — Rhythm Lab

- connect practice, profiles, analysis, reconstruction, conversion, and Chart Doctor into one complete rhythm-game workspace
